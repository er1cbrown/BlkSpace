# Use case — “Not just finance · your yard”

**Persona:** Student (often partner/friend of someone deep in finance literacy) who opens BlkSpace and wonders *what about me?*  

**Product answer:** Same campus OS; **discipline track `social` (Campus life · your yard)** or **`creative`** — not a second-class wallet user.

---

## Story

He lives in Finance & literacy (Hub pro/study, wallet start).  
She wants people, clubs, fashion energy, posts, nights — optional soft WB, never required.

---

## What ships

| Piece | Where |
|-------|--------|
| Track **`social`** | Settings → discipline · `discipline-track.ts` |
| Feed card | `YourYardPathCard` — dismissible CTA |
| Hub reorder | culture · live · fashion · music first |
| Pins | events, clubs, studio, Yard Sale on; literacy off by default |
| Docs | this sheet · capability log |

---

## Honesty

- Not a dating app  
- Not “women only” — any student not in finance mode  
- Soft economy optional  
- Tier 0 eligible (prefs + UI only)
