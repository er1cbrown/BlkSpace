# Super BlkSpace Fighters Series — HBCU Flash Nostalgia Trilogy

**Codename:** ProjectB · **Internal series id:** XK20  
**Umbrella name:** ProjectBlackSpaceFighters  
**Player-facing series:** Super BlkSpace Fighters (SBF)

```
ProjectB(XK20) = ProjectBlackSpaceFighters
```

Three **homebrew** fighting games, same HBCU yard universe, three different **Flash-era nostalgia classes**.  
Hosted on BlkSpace **Yard Arcade + WeixNet** as playable jams / expansion-pack downloads — not Steam, not licensed NG/Nintendo/CF assets.

> **IP rule (non-negotiable):** *Mechanical lineage* and *era energy* only.  
> No Newgrounds icons, no Nintendo cast, no Capoeira Fighter sprites/names, no Riot champions.  
> Roster = original campus personas + **HBCU-inspired mascot fighters** (original designs).

---

## Vision in one paragraph

Like Riot’s **Project L → 2XKO** path (a full fighter with **tag** as the expansion-defining mechanic), the HBCU series grows from a small mascot brawler into a platform fighter, then into a **culture-movement tag fighter** that feels like the best Flash nostalgia (Capoeira Fighter’s craft + CF2/CF3 tag teams + MUGEN “host my cast” energy) — all runnable as **homebrew on BlkSpace**, not browser-abandoned Flash left to die.

## Deep-research anchors (what we steal *feel* from, not content)

### 1) Newgrounds Rumble (2007) — *class for Game 1*

| Trait | Flash classic | Our take |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Cast size | ~12–14 NG icons | **Fewest** campus archetypes |
| Genre | 2D platform-fighter / arena brawler | Same class, original moves |
| Energy | “King of the Portal” mascot chaos | “King of the Yard” club night |
| Modes | Story scenarios, versus up to 4 | Story · Versus · Survival · Challenge |
| Feel | Mash combos, specials, Grounds Gold economy | Soft **WB** + spirit pins, not NG Gold |

*Feels like:* Tank Award–era Newgrounds party fighter.  
*Is not:* Pico, Hank, Alien Hominid, Tankman, etc.

### 2) Super Smash Flash / SSF2 — *class for Game 2*

| Trait | Flash classic | Our take |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Win condition | % damage → knock off stage | Platform fighter rules |
| Movement | Free aerial / ledge / multi-jump growth in SSF2 | **2.0 = better platforming** |
| Roster | Huge crossover cast | **More characters** than Game 1 |
| Feel | Browser Smash for kids who never owned a Wii | Super BlkSpace Fighters 2.0 |

*Feels like:* SSF1→SSF2 jump in depth (engine, specials, stages).  
*Is not:* Nintendo IP or McLeodGaming assets.

### 3) Capoeira Fighter (Scott Stoddard) + tag lineage — *class for Game 3*

| Trait | Flash classic | Our take |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Culture-first kit | Capoeira student authenticity, Portuguese terms, real movement craft | **School-inspired culture norms** + historical/cultural movement vocab (respectful, original) |
| Progression CF1→CF3 | Identical pants → characters → arcade story + **tag teams** | Series ends at **tag-first** design |
| Buttons / stance | Punch/kick planes, stamina, stylish strings | Distinct ground game vs Games 1–2 |
| Tag | CF tag team + later Legacy tag rebinds | **Full tag expansion pack** |

Capoeira Fighter 3 is widely remembered as a peak **Flash fighter** (archive-era ~3MB SWF class, 2v2 tag influence, deep move identity). Legacy Edition work today (60 FPS rebalance) proves the *craft* still matters — we honor that craft with **original** kits.

### 4) 2XKO / Project L (Riot) — *tag product model for Game 3*

| Trait | 2XKO | Our take |
|-------|------|----------|
| Structure | 2v2 point + assist | Duo of **HBCU mascot staples** |
| Assists | Assist actions, charge assist | School-flavor assists (band, step, yard callouts — original) |
| Tags | Handshake tag, swap while both on screen | Tag-in as **headline mechanic** of 3.0 |
| Ship model | Full free fighter, tag is the game | **Expansion-pack full download** on Arcade `size:full` |

Tag can ship **as a separate downloadable pack** that requires 2.0 roster unlocks, or as standalone 3.0 with series continuity.

### 5) MUGEN (Flash nostalgia host culture)

MUGEN’s student culture was: **bring your cast, host nights, share builds**.  
On BlkSpace that maps to:

- Yard Arcade publish + Play shell  
- WeixNet pin nights (club ticket + Hub playable)  
- Optional future “series roster packs” (still original art only)  
- **Not** dumping copyrighted MUGEN chars / commercial SWFs into the product

## Mechanical progression (why three games, not one bloated build)

```
1.0  Rumble-class     → small cast, HP brawls, party chaos, club bracket nights
        ↓
2.0  Smash Flash-class → platforming, % stocks, larger cast, better air game
        ↓
3.0  Capoeira + tag    → grounded cultural movement, assists, handshake-style tag-in,
                         expansion-pack download, mascot staples, WeixNet host nights
```

**Why not one game?** Same reason CF grew CF1→CF3 and SSF grew into SSF2: each *era* of Flash nostalgia had a different skill fantasy. Students who only want “four friends smash on the quad” stay on 1.0/2.0; club fighters who want **tag theory** open 5DXQ.

## HBCU mascot + culture design (Game 3 focus)

| Pillar | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| **Staple mascots** | Original fighter designs inspired by university spirit (tigers, panthers, etc.) — **not** trademarked official logos/art without license |
| **Culture movement** | Movement language inspired by real diasporic / campus culture (dance, step, band, parade, lab grind) with **research + respect**, not caricature |
| **Capoeira *class*** | Circular footwork, ginga-like stance cycles, stamina-as-rhythm — **original** names and animations |
| **School norms** | Stage hazards and win quotes that feel like yard life (quiet library, homecoming lights, lab spill) |
| **Tag as social** | Duo = roommate / rival school / band + athlete pairings — Connect spine, not only 1v1 |

Legal note: trademarked official university marks need rights for commercial use. Product path: **original BlkSpace spirit mascots** themed to yards (TSU / Howard / Meharry / Fisk flavor) until any formal partnership.

### Recommended tech path (forever-fit with Yard stack)

1. **Author** games as open homebrew (Godot 4 export WASM, or Phaser/Pixi jam).  
2. **Publish** to Yard Arcade with `[[size:micro|tier0|full]]` honesty tags.  
3. **Play** via existing Play shell sandbox.  
4. **Social layer** = BlkSpace only: brackets, spirit pins, Club Inc., share cards, WeixNet announce.  
5. **3.0 tag pack** = separate Full download entry linked from 2.0 profile (expansion model like Project L’s identity: tag *is* the product).  
6. **No AI required** for gameplay. No client SpaceXAI.

Tier 0 stays viable if **1.0 micro/tier0** ships first; **3.0 full** is pin-night / lab machine friendly.
