# BlkSpace hybrid (lock)

**Product:** a **customizable Facebook + Instagram + Threads hybrid**, with **Myspace / Newgrounds** soul. Usable as **mobile social media**.  
**Not:** an Instagram clone. **Not:** a TikTok FYP app. **Not:** “think Instagram for HBCU.”

Welcome copy and ranking should follow this file, not Reels-first competitors.

---

## What the hybrid is

| Borrow | What we take | What we do **not** take |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | Campus as group, wall, friends, longer posts, events, marketplace | News-feed ads, global graph |
| **Instagram** | Photo grid on profile, stories ring, visual posts | Reels-as-home, opaque FYP, camera-only culture |
| **Threads** | Text conversation, simple composer, replies in the open | Being a Twitter-news firehose |
| **Myspace** | Themes, music, top friends, the page is *yours* | 2005 HTML injection |
| **Newgrounds** | Creative uploads, arcade/portal, ratings/karma | Flash as a requirement |

**Home** is a **yard wall**: mix of text (Threads) + photos (IG) + campus (Facebook).  
**Profile** is a **customizable page** (Myspace) with a **grid** (IG).  
**Arcade / Hub** is the **portal** (Newgrounds).  
**Watch** is a side room, like IG Reels tucked under Instagram — **not** the product.

---

## What a post is (this model)

Not “a TikTok.” A BlkSpace post is a **wall item** a phone thumb can make:

1. **Note** — text, Threads-style (who’s at the dining hall, hot take, joke)  
2. **Pic / dump** — photo or GIF on the wall + grid  
3. **Talk** — reply on the post (Facebook/Threads)  
4. **Page flair** — story 24h, theme, song (Myspace/IG)  
5. **Upload** — mix, comic, game, file (Newgrounds)

Same storage can stay “text + files + town.” The **product** still has those five *intents* in the composer, not one “caption your video” FYP.

Empty yard: **starter wall cards** (prompts to post a note/pic), not “upload a reel to seed Watch.”

---

## Mobile social (usable)

This hybrid only counts if it works in a pocket:

| Must work on a phone | Why |
|---|---|
| Guest → Home wall in one tap | Facebook/Threads habit |
| Composer: text + camera-roll photo | IG/FB post |
| Bottom nav: Home, Yards, You | Not 12 tabs |
| Profile: grid + customize | Myspace + IG |
| Thumb-sized like/reply | Threads |

Camera-from-app and Watch can wait. **Wall + pic + page** cannot.

---

## Ranking (not an IG FYP)

Yard-local, friends + campus first, substance over farm. Transparent. **No buy-rank.** That is Facebook-group + Threads, not For You.
